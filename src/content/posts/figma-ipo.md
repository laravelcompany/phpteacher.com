**Figma just rang the NYSE bell—and the design world is losing its mind.**  
Is this the triumph of web-based collaboration, or the beginning of the end?

> *“Looks like a pivot from devs to normies.”*  
> *—HN commenter side-eyeing Figma’s CLI-less homepage*

But let’s be real: **Figma won because it bet on the web when Sketch doubled down on Mac.** Now, with a $33/share IPO, the question isn’t *if* things will change—it’s *how fast*.

---  

### 🚀 **How Figma Ate the Design World**

**The winning formula:**
- **Web-first, no-install chaos**: Share a link, not a .sketch file.
- **Viral licensing**: Edit a file? Congrats, you’re now a “seat.”
- **Plugins & sandboxes**: WebAssembly speed + QuickJS security.

**Meanwhile, Sketch was busy:**
- Mac-only.
- No real-time collab.
- *“Just download this 500MB app to view my mockup.”*

---  

### 🔥 **The HN Debate: IPO or RIP-O?**

#### **1. “This is peak enshittification”**
- *“Charging for variables? Really?”*
- *“Wait for the ‘Enterprise Figma AI’ tier.”*
- **Prediction**: 180 days post-IPO, stock tanks.

#### **2. “Nah, Figma’s untouchable”**
- Network effects > everything.
- *“Adobe XD couldn’t kill it. AI won’t either.”*
- **Reality check**: 70% of the world doesn’t use Macs.

#### **3. The AI Wildcard**
- **Figma’s move**: Bake AI into the design pipeline.
- **Devs sweating**: *“If Figma spits React code, do we even need engineers?”*
- **Skeptics**: *“Adobe tried this with Dreamweaver. Look how that turned out.”*

---  

### 💼 **Who’s Cashing Out?**
- **Employees**: Locked up for 6 months (then 💸).
- **VCs**: Printing money since 2012.
- **Users**: Bracing for *“growth-focused”* pricing.

---  

### 🛠️ **The Alternatives (Because Open Source Never Dies)**
- **Penpot**: *“Figma, but OSS”*—lacks polish.
- **Lunacy**: Shockingly good for a stock-photo side project.
- **Inkscape/Krita**: For masochists who love .svg battles.

---  

### 🎤 **Final Take**
Figma’s IPO isn’t an endpoint—it’s a **stress test**.

- **Win**: Stay collaborative, open, and *fast*.
- **Lose**: Pull an Adobe, squeeze users, and watch AI eat your lunch.

> *“The best design tool is the one everyone already uses.”*  
> *—Someone who’s tired of arguing about Figma vs. Sketch*
